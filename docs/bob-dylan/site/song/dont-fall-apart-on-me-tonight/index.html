Infidels · 1983

Don't Fall Apart on Me Tonight

It closes the album.

love

The words

A tired man pleads with a lover to hold together with him for one more night.

Late at night, a tired and uncertain man talks to the woman beside him, admitting regrets about the life he has led and the person he might have been. He wonders aloud about what has passed and what might still come, but keeps circling back to one simple request that she stay strong with him until morning. The mood is tender, worn and a little wry. It closes the album on a quiet, intimate note.
