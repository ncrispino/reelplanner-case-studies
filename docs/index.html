Case studies

reelplanning, used on real projects

reelplanning turns a coding agent's plan into a short narrated video that stops at each open question, so you review the plan by watching it. Each study here follows one project from its first prompt to its last review: what the owner asked, the videos they watched, what they said, and what changed.
